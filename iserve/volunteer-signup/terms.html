Portfolio demo — placeholder content. This build runs on local mock data and does not collect, store, or transmit any real personal information.

1. About this demo

iServe is shown here as a frontend-only portfolio project. Accounts you create exist only in your own browser's local storage and are cleared with the "Reset Demo Data" button. No live backend is involved.

2. Acceptable use

In the real product, volunteers agree to provide accurate profile information, to treat organizers and other volunteers respectfully, and to honour event commitments they sign up for.

3. Volunteer participation

Joining an event through iServe creates a request that the organizing group reviews and approves. Organizers may set skill or availability requirements for their events.

4. Privacy

The production service would share a volunteer's name, contact details, and listed skills with an organizer only after the volunteer requests to join that organizer's event.
