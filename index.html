About

Full-stack, across the stack — and across platforms.

Three automations. Three business problems. One toolkit: Zapier.

A Zapier automation that turns a dropped-in mp3 into published social content: detects a new upload in a Google Drive folder, filters for audio files, transcribes with AI, then generates two distinct blog posts (title + content pairs). Paths splits the two variants so each one is auto-posted to LinkedIn, Facebook, or Instagram.

A status-driven CRM automation in Asana: a task's Status field fans out via Paths into five lifecycle workflows — lead-folder and subtask creation, timed follow-up sequences with response checks, a quote follow-up sequence, an AI-drafted onboarding email with invoice attachment, and an AI-drafted thank-you email on close.

Lead Enrichment & Routing

A webhook-driven lead-qualification pipeline: captures form submissions, parses the lead's email domain, enriches the company via Apollo, then uses AI to analyze company size and founding year.
