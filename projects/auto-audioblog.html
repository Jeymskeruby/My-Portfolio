Audio-to-Blog Social Publisher

Drop an audio file in a Google Drive folder and Zapier turns it into two ready-to-post blog articles, automatically published to social media — no manual writing or copy-pasting.

ZapierGoogle Drive AI by ZapierPaths Social publishing

Overview

Content teams record audio (voice notes, interview snippets, quick updates) and need it turned into publish-ready blog posts and social copy — normally a manual transcription-and-writing job. This automation removes that step entirely: dropping an mp3 into a watched Google Drive folder is the only human action required. From there, Zapier transcribes the audio, drafts two distinct blog-post options with AI, and posts each one to social media on its own path.

How it works

  1. Trigger — Google Drive "Detect New File in Folder," polling the target directory every 2 minutes.
  2. Filter — only continues if the uploaded file has an .mp3 extension.
  3. Transcribe — AI by Zapier converts the audio file to text.
  4. Generate — AI by Zapier drafts two separate blog posts from the transcript, producing four outputs: Title A, Content A, Title B, Content B.
  5. Paths — the workflow splits into two independent branches, one per blog variant.
  6. Path A — checks that Title A exists, then posts Title A + Content A to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, or another connected social account.
  7. Path B — checks that Title B exists, then posts Title B + Content B to a social account, independently of Path A.

Why it's built this way

  • The mp3 filter keeps the automation safe to point at a shared folder — non-audio uploads are ignored rather than erroring out.
  • Generating two blog variants per recording gives the content team a choice, or lets both go out as separate posts, without any extra manual work.
  • Splitting into two Paths after generation means either branch can be edited, retargeted to a different platform, or disabled independently without touching the other.
