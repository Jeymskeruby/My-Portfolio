Lead Enrichment & Routing

Every inbound lead is automatically enriched with company data, AI-qualified, logged, and routed to the right internal notification — no manual research required.

ZapierWebhooks ApolloAI by Zapier Google SheetsSlack

Overview

Lead forms capture only the basics (name, email, company, source) — but a sales team needs company context to know which leads to chase first. This automation takes a raw form submission, enriches it with real company data, uses AI to judge whether it's a high-priority lead, and gets it logged and in front of the team within seconds of submission.

How it works

  1. Trigger — a Webhook receives any lead-gathering form submission: first name, last name, email, company name, company size, website, lead source.
  2. Format — a Formatter step extracts the domain from the lead's email address.
  3. Enrich — a Webhook (GET) call to Apollo looks up the company by domain and pulls back company data.
  4. Analyze — AI by Zapier reads the Apollo data and extracts the company's real size and founding year.
  5. Paths — the workflow branches into High Priority and Fallback.
  6. High Priority path — condition: company size and founding year both qualify the lead as high priority.
    • Google Sheets (or another database) logs the lead.
    • An internal notification goes out via Slack/Gmail.
  7. Fallback path — every lead that doesn't qualify as high priority.
    • Google Sheets (or another database) logs the lead.
    • An internal notification goes out via Slack/Gmail.

Why it's built this way

  • Enriching before qualifying means the priority decision is based on real company data, not just whatever the lead typed into the form.
  • Every lead is logged and surfaced internally regardless of priority — the Paths branch changes urgency, not visibility, so nothing silently falls through.
  • A Webhook trigger keeps the automation form-agnostic — any form or landing page that can POST to it plugs straight into the pipeline.
