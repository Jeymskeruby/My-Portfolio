Asana CRM Lead Pipeline

A single Custom Field on an Asana task drives an entire client lifecycle — onboarding, follow-ups, quoting, approval, and thank-you — with no manual email chasing.

ZapierAsana GmailGoogle Drive AI by ZapierDelay / Filter logic

Overview

Asana is used as a lightweight CRM: each lead/client is a task, and a Status custom field tracks where they are in the pipeline. Instead of a team member manually acting on every status change — creating folders, sending follow-ups, chasing quotes, onboarding, and thanking clients — this automation watches for status changes and runs the right sequence automatically, branching into one of five paths.

How it works

Trigger: Asana "Update Task," filtered to changes on the Status custom field. Paths then split into five branches, one per status value.

  1. Ready to Start — condition: Status exactly matches "Ready to Start."
    • Google Drive creates a lead folder, named after the Asana task name.
    • Asana creates a "Social Media Content" subtask under the main task.
  2. No Response — condition: Status exactly matches "No Response."
    • Gmail sends a follow-up email using the task's email data.
    • Delay 2 days.
    • Gmail "Find Email" checks whether the client replied.
    • Filter checks whether that condition is met.
    • Gmail sends a second follow-up email if the client still hasn't responded.
  3. Quoted — condition: Status exactly matches "Quoted."
    • Gmail sends a price-quote email using the task's email data.
    • Delay 2 days.
    • Gmail "Find Email" checks whether the client replied to the quote.
    • Filter checks whether that condition is met.
    • Gmail sends a quote follow-up email if the client still hasn't responded.
  4. Approved — condition: Status exactly matches "Approved."
    • AI by Zapier drafts an onboarding email tailored to the client.
    • Google Drive finds the client's invoice document.
    • Gmail sends the AI-drafted onboarding email with the invoice attached.
  5. Paid and Closed — condition: Status exactly matches "Paid and Closed."
    • AI by Zapier drafts a thank-you email tailored to the client.
    • Gmail sends the thank-you email to the client.

Why it's built this way

  • Driving everything off one Custom Field keeps the whole pipeline visible and editable from inside Asana — no separate system to check.
  • The Delay + "Find Email" + Filter pattern in the No Response and Quoted paths automates realistic follow-up cadences without ever double-emailing a client who already replied.
  • AI-drafted onboarding and thank-you emails stay tailored per client while removing the repetitive writing work at the two moments that matter most for tone.
